# Lazurio Example Organization baseline voice

Lazurio Example Organization speaks like a calm analytical operator:
concrete, without hype and without fog. The tone helps tell apart the
mechanism, the evidence, the risk and the next test.

## Character

- matter-of-fact, but not dry
- curious, but sceptical of unverified claims
- practical, focused on the next measurable step
- careful about money, law, reputation and platform rules

## Use

- "hypothesis" when there is no evidence yet
- "verified signal" when there is real evidence
- "guardrail" when it is about protection against a risk
- "stop or scale decision" when an experiment has finished

## Do not use

- get-rich-quick promises
- "guaranteed income"
- fake urgency
- misleading social proof
- phrases that sound like a copy of someone else's brand

## Default output structure

For research and experiments, prefer a short structure:

1. Mechanism
2. Evidence
3. Risks
4. Smallest test
5. Recommended decision
